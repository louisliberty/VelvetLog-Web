Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Your Privacy is Our Priority

VelvetLog is designed with privacy-first principles. Your personal relationship data is encrypted, secure, and completely under your control.

End-to-End Encryption

All your data is encrypted before it leaves your device using AES-256 encryption.

Zero Knowledge

We cannot see your data. Only you have the keys to decrypt your information.

Data Portability

Export your data anytime. You own your information completely.

Detailed Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, name (optional), authentication credentials

Relationship Data: All contact information, timeline entries, and reminders you create

Technical Data: Device information for security purposes, encrypted locally

We DO NOT collect: Your actual relationship data (it's encrypted), browsing history outside our app, or any personally identifiable information about your contacts

2. How We Protect Your Data

Local-First Storage: Your data is stored primarily on your device

End-to-End Encryption: All data is encrypted with AES-256 before transmission

Zero-Knowledge Architecture: We cannot decrypt or access your personal data

Secure Authentication: Biometric authentication and strong password requirements

Data Integrity: Cryptographic hashing ensures your data hasn't been tampered with

3. Cloud Backup (Optional)

Opt-in Only: Cloud backup is disabled by default

Encrypted Storage: If enabled, data is encrypted before upload to cloud storage

Your Keys: Only you have the decryption keys, not us or cloud providers

Geographic Control: Choose your preferred data center location

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We NEVER share your personal relationship data with anyone

Analytics: Only anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (opt-out available)

Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure providers may store encrypted data but cannot decrypt it

Legal Requests: We can only provide encrypted data, which is useless without your keys

5. Your Rights and Controls

Data Export: Download all your data in standard formats anytime

Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated data

Privacy Settings: Control what minimal data is shared for app functionality

Backup Control: Enable/disable cloud backup at any time

Data Retention: Set how long to keep your data locally

6. Security Measures

Authentication: Biometric authentication, strong passwords, optional 2FA

Encryption: AES-256 encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation

Data Integrity: Cryptographic signatures prevent data tampering

Secure Transmission: All data transmission uses TLS 1.3

Local Security: Data is encrypted even when stored locally on your device

7. Contact and Updates

Privacy Questions: Contact us at privacy@velvetlog.app

Policy Updates: You'll be notified of any significant changes

Transparency: Our security practices are regularly audited
