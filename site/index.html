Frequently asked questions

What is VelvetLog?

VelvetLog is a private relationship management app that helps you keep track of your connections, set reminders for important dates, and log interactions — all with complete discretion and privacy.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. VelvetLog features biometric app lock, discreet notifications, and zero data sharing with third parties. Your personal life stays personal.

How much does it cost?

VelvetLog is free to use with up to 5 connections. Our Membership plan at $7.99/month unlocks unlimited connections, AI suggestions, photo uploads, and more. Every membership starts with a 7-day free trial.

Is it available on iPhone and Android?

VelvetLog is launching on iPhone first and will be on the App Store soon. An Android version on Google Play will follow.

Can I import my existing contacts?

Yes, VelvetLog supports importing contacts directly from your phone. You can select which contacts to import and add additional details like relationship type, notes, and more.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

If you cancel your Membership, you'll keep access to all features until the end of your billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan with up to 5 connections. Your data is never deleted.
